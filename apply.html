How to apply

  1. Click Continue with your UMU email and sign in with Google using your university address.
  2. Only a real @umu.ac.ug or @stud.umu.ac.ug Google account is accepted — invented emails will not work.
  3. Fill in your name, phone, hostel, and faculty, then submit. An admin will review your application unless auto-approve is on.

Password accounts are not used. Your university Google login is your identity on this site.
