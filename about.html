Our story

How it started

The Nkobazambogo Students' Association grew from a simple need: students at Uganda Martyrs University wanted a dependable way to stay linked: not only for cultural belonging, but for practical, everyday solidarity. Shared activities, project collaboration, and fair elections for executive roles became the backbone of that idea.

Today the association operates as a membership body. Applications from a @umu.ac.ug email are reviewed by an admin before an account is activated. Once approved, members can join club activities, take part in projects, stand or vote in elections, and take part in discussions.

Governance

Who manages what

Day-to-day member approvals and content creation are handled by association admins. Sensitive actions: permanent account deletion, assigning admin access, editing club-wide settings, adding members outside the @umu.ac.ug domain, and closing elections: are reserved for the Super Admin.
