Is this your first time here?

To sign in to the Nkobazambogo Students' Association portal, please follow these steps:

  1. Click the "Login with your UMU email" button.
  2. You will be redirected to a Google sign-in page. Enter your university email address (e.g. name@stud.umu.ac.ug or name@umu.ac.ug) and password, then click Sign in.
  3. Once you have successfully signed in, you will be taken back to this site and should have access to member features.

Only university emails on umu.ac.ug (including student addresses) are accepted. Your membership must also be approved by an admin.
