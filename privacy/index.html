Privacy Policy

This explains what member data the Nkobazambogo Students' Association website collects, why, how it's protected, and the choices you have.

Terms of Service Members' Code & Principles
Document: Nkobazambogo Students' Association Privacy Policy Version: 1.0 · 2026 Applies to: Visitors, applicants, and members
Plain-language summary. We collect the information needed to run membership approval, activities, projects, elections, the forum, and admin functions for the Nkobazambogo Students' Association at UMU Nkozi. We don't sell your data. Access inside the system is restricted by role, and your individual votes are never visible to anyone - not even Super Admins - once cast. Ask to delete your account at any time and we will delete it, in full, no questions asked.

1. Who controls your data

The Nkobazambogo Students' Association (NSA), a students' association of Uganda Martyrs University, Nkozi, is the data controller for information collected through this website. Day-to-day administration is carried out by elected/appointed admins and the Super Admin on the association's behalf. Contact us via the Contact page or nsa@umu.ac.ug with any privacy question.

3. How we use your information

  1. To review and approve (or decline) membership applications restricted to @umu.ac.ug addresses.
  2. To run the member directory, activities, projects, news, forum, and feedback features you choose to use.
  3. To run leadership elections: verifying eligibility to vote or stand, recording candidate applications, and recording votes as described in Section 5.
  4. To respond to contact messages and support requests.
  5. To keep the Site secure: detecting suspicious sign-in activity, locking accounts after repeated failed attempts, and maintaining an audit trail of admin actions.
  6. To communicate important association updates (e.g. election openings, approval decisions) related to your membership.

4. Who can see your information

  1. Other members can generally see basic profile information (name, avatar, programme) where the Site is designed to show it - for example, in the member directory, forum posts, or leadership listings, if you hold a leadership position.
  2. Admins can see membership and profile data needed to review applications, moderate content, and run activities, projects, and elections. Admin access is role-based and logged.
  3. The Super Admin has the broadest access, including assigning admin roles and, where necessary, permanently deleting an account and its associated data.
  4. Nobody - including admins and the Super Admin - can see how an individual member voted; see Section 5.
  5. We do not sell member data, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

5. How we protect election data

Ballot secrecy is enforced at the database level, not just by policy. When you vote, your choice is recorded together with your account so the system can prevent double-voting and support an audit if a result is ever disputed. However, the underlying access rules only ever let you look up whether you have voted for a given position - no admin, including the Super Admin, has a way to read who any individual member voted for through the system. Once cast, a vote record cannot be altered or deleted by anyone. Only aggregated results are published.

6. Where your data is stored

  1. Member data, profiles, activity, and election records are stored using Supabase (a hosted PostgreSQL database and authentication provider), with access controlled by row-level security rules tied to your account and role.
  2. Profile photos (avatars) are stored in Supabase Storage; you can only upload or replace files inside your own account's folder (admins may remove an inappropriate photo).
  3. Passwords are managed by Supabase Auth and are never stored or visible to us in plain text.
  4. The Site itself is hosted on Netlify.

7. Data retention

  1. We keep membership and activity data for as long as your account is active, or until you ask us to delete it - whichever comes first. Deletion requests are honored in full; see Section 8.
  2. Security logs (sign-in attempts) and the admin audit trail are retained for security and accountability purposes while your account exists, and are removed along with your account on deletion, except where an entry no longer identifies you personally (e.g. an aggregate count).
  3. Contact messages are retained to allow follow-up, but will be deleted on request in the same way as other personal data.

8. Your choices & rights

  1. You can review and update most of your profile information (phone, programme, year of study, bio, avatar) at any time from your account.
  2. You can ask an admin to correct inaccurate information you cannot edit yourself.
  3. You can request that your account and associated personal data be permanently deleted at any time, for any reason, by asking any admin or the Super Admin. On request, your account, profile, and personal data are permanently deleted from the system - no retention period, no approval needed beyond confirming it's really you, and no exceptions.
  4. You can ask what personal data we hold about you by contacting us.
  5. You can decline to provide optional information (such as a bio, phone number, or security question), though this may limit some account-recovery or member-directory features.

9. Children's privacy

This Site is intended for university students and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the Site's features change. Material changes will be announced through official association channels (such as the news page or a site announcement), and the version date at the top of this page will be updated.

11. Contact us

For any question about this policy or your data, reach us through the Contact page or at nsa@umu.ac.ug.
