Who we are

A home away from home at UMU Nkozi

The Nkobazambogo Students' Association exists to keep UMU students connected: through club activities, shared projects, mentorship, and a say in who leads the association each term. Membership is by approval: every application from a umu.ac.ug email is reviewed by an admin before an account becomes active.

Vision

A lasting community of UMU students who show up for one another, on and off campus.

Mission

Connect and empower members through club activities, projects, mentorship, and fair elections.

Principles

Integrity, solidarity, and respect - set out in our Members' Code for everyone to read.
