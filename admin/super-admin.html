Bypasses the @umu.ac.ug self-registration restriction: use this for anyone who should have an account on a different email domain. The account is created active and approved immediately.

Club Settings

Shown on the public About page and elsewhere. Only the Super Admin can edit these.

Close an election & promote winners

Closing an election locks in the vote counts and promotes the top candidate per position to the public Leadership page. This can't be undone from the UI.

Feedback forms

Deactivating or deleting an existing survey is restricted to the Super Admin to protect data already collected.

Permanently delete a member account

This removes the person's login and profile entirely. It cannot be undone.
